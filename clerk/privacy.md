# clerk privacy policy

clerk is used only by me, on my own Google account. It reads the Gmail messages I label and saves their invoices to my Google Drive. The data stays in my Google account and on my computer, and is never shared or sold. Its use of Google user data follows the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

clerk also reads the balances and transactions of my own bank accounts through Enable Banking, an account information service provider regulated by the Finnish Financial Supervisory Authority. I authorise this access myself in my bank's app and can revoke it at any time. The bank data passes through Enable Banking and is stored only in my Google account and on my computer, never shared or sold.
