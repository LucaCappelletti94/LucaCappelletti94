# clerk privacy policy

clerk is used by Luca Cappelletti alone, on his own Google account. It has no other users.

**Data accessed.** Gmail messages carrying the labels Luca assigns, with their attachments, through the `gmail.modify` scope. Files in the Google Drive folder he designates and the spreadsheet kept there, through the `drive` scope.

**Use.** clerk saves invoices and receipts from those messages into that Drive folder, records them in the spreadsheet, and changes the labels of the messages it has handled. It does nothing else with the data.

**Storage.** The sign-in tokens are stored on Luca's own computer. Message contents are processed on that computer and stored only in his own Google Drive. Short notices about filed documents are sent to his own Telegram chat.

**Sharing.** No data is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. clerk's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

**Contact.** cappelletti.luca94@gmail.com
