# clerk

clerk is a program Luca Cappelletti runs on his own computer to keep the paperwork of his Italian VAT registration (partita IVA) in order. It is not offered to anyone else.

Signed in to Luca's own Google account, clerk reads the Gmail messages he labels as invoices, saves their invoices and receipts into a folder of his Google Drive, records them in a spreadsheet in that folder, and moves each handled message to a "filed" or "to review" label.
